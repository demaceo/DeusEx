The deepfake-prevalence and provenance material — Sumsub's tenfold rise, Europol's 90%
projection, the Content Authenticity Initiative, C2PA, the EU AI Act's transparency article, and
the _Nature_ model-collapse paper — all check out. The single failure is one figure that appears
twice (a stat box and its companion donut): the "24% can spot a deepfake" number is real but is
**misattributed to iProov (2022)** when it actually comes from a 2020 academic paper.

## ⚠️ Disputed (2)

- **`s-v-iproov-detection`** and **`c-v-iproov-chart`** — "24% could reliably tell a high-quality
  deepfake video from a real one (iProov, 2022)." The ~24.5% figure originates from **Korshunov &
  Marcel (2020), "Deepfake detection: humans vs. machines"** (arXiv:2009.03155), a subjective
  study — not from iProov. iProov's actual 2022 survey measured _self-reported confidence_ (57%
  _think_ they could spot a deepfake), and iProov's real detection-rate study is from 2025 (only
  0.1% correctly identified all synthetic media). The number is credible; the source and year are
  wrong. The claims are re-pointed to the Korshunov & Marcel paper and flagged disputed.
  Source: [arXiv:2009.03155](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.03155)

## ✅ Verified (9) — highlights

- **`s-v-sumsub-10x`** / **`c-v-sumsub-chart`** — Sumsub reported a ~10x rise in detected deepfake
  fraud attempts from 2022 to 2023; the claim correctly frames it as a detected-pipeline figure.
  [Sumsub](https://sumsub.com/newsroom/sumsub-research-global-deepfake-incidents-surge-tenfold-from-2022-to-2023/)
- **`s-v-europol-90`** / **`c-v-europol-chart`** / **`c-v-europol-projection`** — Europol's 2022
  "Facing Reality?" report raised the possibility that up to 90% of online content _may be_
  synthetically generated by 2026; correctly framed as a projection, not a measurement, exactly as
  the Skeptic notes in-text.
- **`c-v-cai-chart`** — the Content Authenticity Initiative (founded Nov 2019 by Adobe, The New
  York Times, and Twitter) grew to several thousand members by 2024; the intermediate 2021 point
  is flagged approximate. [CAI](https://contentauthenticity.org/)
- **`c-v-eu-transparency`** — the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689), Article 50, imposes
  synthetic-content transparency/marking obligations. [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R1689)
- **`c-v-nature-collapse`** — Shumailov et al., "AI models collapse when trained on recursively
  generated data," _Nature_ 631 (2024). [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07566-y)
- **`c-v-c2pa`** — C2PA publishes the open standard for tamper-evident, cryptographically signed
  content credentials. [C2PA](https://c2pa.org/)
